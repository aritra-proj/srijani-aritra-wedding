Auspicious Wedding Rituals

🪔 🌸 🪔

Experience the divine beauty of our traditional Bengali wedding ceremonies as Srijani & Aritra unite through holy vows and rituals.

MORNING RITUAL
🌼

Gaye Holud

গায়ে হলুদ

Morning • 10:30 AM

The joyous ceremony of turmeric paste applied to the bride and groom, symbolizing purity, glowing blessings, sweets, and family songs.

SACRED HIGHLIGHT
👰

Saat Paak & Subho Drishti

সাত পাক ও শুভদৃষ্টি

Auspicious Lagna • 7:45 PM

The bride carried on a wooden Piri circles the groom 7 times, removing betel leaves for their first magical glance, followed by Mala Badal!

THE ETERNAL UNION
🔥

Saat Padi & Sindoor Daan

সিঁদুরদান ও সপ্তপদী

Night • 8:30 PM

Sacred Vedic mantras around the holy Agni, taking seven eternal steps together, and the crowning moment of bright red vermilion!
